Project Goal: Create a useful booking system for a gym that meets the customer's requirements.

As a gym member, I want to book a group training class, so that I can reserve a place in advance.

As a gym member, I want to cancel my booking, so that someone else can take my place if I cannot attend.

As an instructor, I want to see the list of members who have booked my class, so that I know who is attending.

Acceptance Criteria: The gym member should be able to see a list of training classes to choose from after logging in and be able to reserve a booking.
